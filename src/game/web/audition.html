PICK THE SOUNDS

Sound effects are synthesised in the browser, so they cost nothing and need no licence. Each game event in three styles: arcade (square waves, coin-op), neon (soft, with echo), laser (saw sweeps and noise). Music is CC0 from OpenGameArt: free, no payment, no credit required.

WHILE YOU MARK: THE CLOCK IS RUNNING

Each plays one whole series, sped up twice: 40 seconds in 20. It builds as time runs out; the tick-tock of the last 10 seconds and the time-up buzzer play on top, as in the game. Try one inside a real round with /?ambient=sonar.
