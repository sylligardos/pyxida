# Audio

`battle-loop.ogg` is "8-Bit Battle Loop" by Wolfgang_ (Theodore Kerr),
https://opengameart.org/content/8-bit-battle-loop, released under CC0 1.0
(public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Credit is not required; the author suggests "Theodore Kerr" if given.

All sound effects are synthesised in code (`../sfx.js`); there are no other
audio files.
